Daily arXiv Picks

Since February 2014 I have kept a daily list of the arXiv preprints I find interesting, and since October 2017 the list has been public on my website. This page puts all __N__ picks in one searchable place, along with what they show about how my reading has changed. One star (★) marks a paper I found especially interesting; two stars (★★) mark one that is very relevant to my own work.
